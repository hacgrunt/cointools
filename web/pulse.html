What is Pulse?

Pulse detects Solana tokens showing signs of organic accumulation — steady buying across multiple timeframes, volume building gradually, price holding or rising. It targets the $50K–$200K MCap sweet spot where breakout candidates live. Tokens are penalized for pump-and-dump patterns (extreme spikes, heavy sell pressure, price crashes).
